About Bixcart

Bixcart is a student marketplace built to make buying and selling within the campus community simpler, safer, and more accountable.

Buyers can discover products, pay through Bixcart, and use the Paystack Split Payments and order verification workflow. Sellers can list products for free and have their seller share allocated automatically through Paystack Split at checkout.

Bixcart currently serves Ajayi Crowther University and is designed to grow with the student community.
